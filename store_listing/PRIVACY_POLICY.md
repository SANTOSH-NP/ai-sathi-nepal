# Privacy Policy for AI Sathi Nepal

**Last Updated: April 28, 2026**

## Introduction

AI Sathi Nepal ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Name, email address (when you register)
- Google or Apple account details (when using social login)
- Profile photo (optional)

### Usage Data
- Features used and frequency
- AI tool usage counts
- Device type and operating system
- App version

### Content Data
- CV/Resume data you create (stored securely)
- Text submitted to AI tools (processed and not stored permanently)
- Images uploaded for PDF conversion (processed and deleted after conversion)

### Payment Information
- Subscription status
- Transaction IDs
- Payment method used (Khalti, eSewa, Stripe)
- We do NOT store credit card numbers or bank details

## How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain our services
- To process your subscription payments
- To track usage limits for free-tier users
- To improve our AI tools and app features
- To send important notifications about your account
- To prevent fraud and abuse

## Data Storage and Security

- Data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud)
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device
- API keys are protected server-side and never exposed in the app

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Firebase** (Google): Authentication, database, storage
- **OpenAI**: AI text processing (your text is sent to OpenAI for processing)
- **Google AdMob**: Advertisements (free tier only)
- **Khalti/eSewa**: Payment processing (Nepal)
- **Stripe**: International payment processing
- **Google Play Billing / Apple IAP**: In-app purchases

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Delete your account and all associated data
- Export your data
- Opt out of non-essential data collection
- Request corrections to your data

## Data Retention

- Account data is retained while your account is active
- AI processing data is not permanently stored
- Payment records are retained as required by law
- Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days

## Children's Privacy

AI Sathi Nepal is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last Updated" date.
