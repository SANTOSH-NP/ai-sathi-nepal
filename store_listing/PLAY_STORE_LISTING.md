AI Sathi Nepal - Your AI Companion for everyday tasks!

AI Sathi Nepal is a powerful multi-tool mobile app designed specifically for Nepal users. Whether you're a student, job seeker, freelancer, or small business owner, AI Sathi Nepal has the tools you need.

KEY FEATURES:

CV / Resume Builder
- Create professional CVs in Europass, ATS-friendly, and Modern formats
- Edit and preview in real-time
- Export as PDF with one tap
- Perfect for job applications

QR Code Scanner
- Scan QR codes instantly
- Detect URLs, text, WiFi credentials, and contacts
- Smart action suggestions (open, save, copy)

Image to PDF Converter
- Upload multiple images
- Generate A4-sized PDFs
- Compression and quality optimization
- Share PDFs instantly

AI Text Tools (Powered by AI)
- Grammar correction for perfect writing
- Text summarization for quick reading
- Translation between Nepali and English
- Professional text rewriting

Smart Daily Tools
- Email generator for professional communication
- Business caption generator for social media
- Job application message templates

ADDITIONAL FEATURES:
- Bilingual support (English and Nepali)
- Clean, modern, fast design
- Works offline for basic features
- Secure login (Google, Apple, Email, or Guest mode)

SUBSCRIPTION PLANS:
- Free: Access to all tools with daily limits, ads enabled
- Premium: Unlimited usage, no ads, faster AI, priority support

NEPAL PAYMENT SUPPORT:
- Pay with Khalti or eSewa
- International cards via Stripe
- Google Play Billing supported
